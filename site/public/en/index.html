The app, as it is

Three workshops.
One tool.

Ascend started as a self-coaching tool. It now covers the whole improvement cycle: the solo player, the coach following students, the team training together. Everyone only sees what the others chose to share.

  1. I

    The player

    To climb in ranked, alone, with a method.

    • Goal-driven sessions: 1 to 3 fundamentals from 10 families (waves, vision, trading, rotations…).
    • Automatic recording of every game, detected with no clicks.
    • Guided review: per-goal scoring, timestamped notes on the video.
    • Mistakes flagged automatically and placed on the VOD timeline.
    • Statistics per game, laning phase, averages and progression over time.
    • Fundamentals footprint: recent form vs your last 40 games, with support-specific KPIs.
    • Transparent rule-based assistant: it always says why, no paid AI.
    • Multi-account: one history for all your Riot accounts.
  2. II

    The coach

    To follow students, live or remotely.

    • Coaching groups by invitation, with roles (coach, analyst, player).
    • Sharing chosen by the player: reviews, stats or nothing, changeable anytime.
    • Timestamped comments on reviews, coach ↔ player threads.
    • Assigned goals with due dates, picked up in one click next session.
    • Individual development plans and one-on-one tracking.
    • Weekly reports and an alert when a student drops off.
  3. III

    The team

    For amateur and semi-pro rosters who train seriously.

    • Calendar-style schedule, availability and common slots, calendar export.
    • Scrims: preparation, checklist, post-match analysis.
    • Drafts, champion pools and roster synergies.
    • Opponent scouting from the Riot IDs you enter.
    • Team VOD review, debrief transcribed locally.
    • Discord bot and a mobile web app to follow from anywhere.

One loop.
Four moves.

Players who improve don't necessarily play more: they play with a goal, review and measure. Ascend does the tedious work between games.

  1. 01

    Pick a goal

    Ascend suggests one from your recent games. You can change it.

  2. 02

    Play as usual

    The game is detected and recorded. Nothing is shown while you play.

  3. 03

    Review what matters

    Key moments are already marked. Score your goal in two minutes.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Your last 15 games against your last 40: you see whether it works.

In detail

What makes you win.
Not just averages.

The Insights tab reads your last 50 games and answers real questions: which champions you win on, what changes in your play when you lose, when you play best, and whether you tilt after a loss.

  • Wins vs losses: deaths, KDA, farm and vision side by side, with your #1 lever.
  • Tilt check: your win rate right after a loss, in the same session.
  • Honest samples: fewer than 3 games is greyed out. No false certainty.

Your VOD comes
annotated.

After each game, Ascend matches the video with the official match timeline: deaths, objectives, fights. Likely mistakes are marked, with an explanation you confirm or dismiss.

  • Mistake library: every tagged mistake feeds your recurring-mistake stats.
  • Clips and bookmarks to keep a moment, share it or send it to your coach.
  • Your videos stay on your PC. They are never uploaded to our servers.

How Ascend uses
Riot data.

Ascend relies on the official Riot Games API, read-only, and follows Riot's developer policies. Here is exactly what the application requests, and what it never does.

After the game, never during

Ascend shows nothing in game: no overlay, no information about opponents, no live advantage. The game's local Live Client Data API is only used to know when a game starts and ends, to start and stop recording.

The key stays on our servers

The application ships no API key. Requests go through an Ascend relay (HTTPS) that adds the key, only allows a closed list of read-only calls and rate-limits each account.

Match data is cached

Each match is requested once, then kept on your PC. This respects Riot's rate limits and keeps the app fast.

No home-made ranking

Ascend does not estimate MMR and does not replace the official ladder. The rank shown is the one Riot provides.

What you choose to share

Your coaching data stays private. A coach or teammate only sees what you agreed to share with their group, and you can withdraw that at any time.

Public data only

Scouting only uses the public match history of Riot IDs you enter. Ascend does not try to identify anonymous players.

Your data
is yours.

Ascend runs on your PC first. The account is for backups, syncing your devices and working with your coach or team. Read the privacy policy.

Local first

Sessions, reviews, notes and statistics live in the application. It keeps working offline.

Videos never uploaded

Recordings stay on your disk. You decide what you publish (for example on your own YouTube channel).

Hosted in Europe

Accounts and backups at Supabase, EU / Switzerland region. No data sold, no advertising.

Export and deletion

Export all your data to a file. Delete your account and backups in one click, from the app.

Questions

Is Ascend free?

Yes. Every feature described here is free during the beta, and a complete free version will always remain available.

How do I get access?

The beta is invite-only: an access code, or the code of a team or coaching group that invites you. Ask for one at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Is it allowed by Riot Games?

Ascend uses Riot Games' official public API and follows its policies: no game modification, no information during the game, no live advantage. Ascend isn't endorsed by Riot Games (see the notice at the bottom of the page).

Does it work on Mac?

The desktop app is available for Windows 10 and 11. The web app (reviews, coaching, schedule, scrims) works on every device but doesn't record games.

Does recording slow the game down?

Capture uses your graphics card's hardware encoder when available. Quality, frame rate and folder are configurable, and automatic recording can be turned off.

Do my teammates see my statistics?

Only if you decide so. Sharing is set group by group (reviews, statistics, team data, wellbeing) and can be withdrawn anytime.

Is there AI?

Not at the core: the assistant applies transparent coaching rules. Voice transcription of debriefs uses a model that runs on your PC; nothing is sent to an AI service.
