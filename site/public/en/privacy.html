2. The principle: local first

By default, your sessions, reviews, notes, scores, statistics and video recordings stay in the application, on your computer. The Ascend account is used to back this data up, sync it across your devices and, if you choose, work with a coach or a team.

4. What never leaves your computer

  • Videos of your games, clips and thumbnails.
  • The Riot match cache and detailed statistics computed from it.
  • Debrief audio: transcription is done by a model running on your PC.
  • Your YouTube access: if you publish a video to your channel, the authorization is encrypted by the Windows keychain and the upload goes directly from your PC to YouTube.
  • Personal Discord webhooks and per-PC settings (recording folder, quality, appearance).

5. Who sees what

  • Only you, by default.
  • A group's staff (coach, analyst, manager) sees your reviews and statistics only if you share those categories with that group.
  • Your teammates see team data (schedule, scrims, drafts, published champion pools), never your personal reviews.
  • You can leave a group or withdraw sharing at any time; access stops immediately.

6. Hosting providers and recipients

  • Supabase — accounts, sync, backups, group features. Hosted in the European Union / Switzerland (Zurich region).
  • Cloudflare — Riot Games API relay, web app, Discord bot and this website (global network).
  • Riot Games — receives the necessary API requests, under its own privacy policy.
  • Discord and YouTube (Google) — only if you use those integrations.
  • GitHub — hosts the installer files; this website queries GitHub's public API to show the latest version.

No data is sold or used for advertising. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.

7. Retention

  • Cloud backups: the 10 most recent per account; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Account, synced data and group data: until you delete your account.
  • Riot relay technical logs: the hosting provider's standard retention; Ascend does not store the requests.
  • Bug reports: until handled, and deleted on request.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings → Backup exports all your data to a .nexusmind file.
  • Erasure: Settings → Ascend account → Delete my account immediately deletes your account, synced data and cloud backups. Data on your PC is not touched: to erase it, uninstall the application, then delete its data folder (under %APPDATA%) and your recordings folder.
  • Rectification, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent: write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We answer within one month.
  • Complaint: you can contact your country's data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

9. Minors

Ascend is intended for people aged 15 and over. Below that age, using an account requires the agreement of a parent or legal guardian.

10. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), desktop session protected by the Windows keychain, row-level access control on the server (everyone only reads what is meant for or shared with them), Riot API key never present in the application.

11. Changes

This policy may change with the application. Significant changes will be announced in the app before they apply.
