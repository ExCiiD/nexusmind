1. The service

Ascend is an improvement tool for League of Legends published by ExCiiD. It includes a Windows desktop application, a web application and online services: account, backup and sync, access to game data through the Riot Games API, coaching and team features, and a Discord bot.

The service is currently an invite-only beta: features may change, be interrupted or contain errors.

2. Price

Ascend is free. If paid options ever appear, they will be optional and announced in advance, and a free version will remain available.
