Privacy Policy

Version 0.3 · updated November 19, 2026 · applies to the desktop application, the web app and this website

1. Who is responsible for your data

Ascend is published by ExCiiD, Réunion, France. Contact for any question about your data: exciid501@gmail.com.

2. The principle: local first

By default, your sessions, reviews, notes, scores, statistics and video recordings stay in the application, on your computer. The Ascend account is used to back this data up, sync it across your devices and, if you choose, work with a coach or a team.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Wellbeing check-ins are health data: both their storage and their sharing with a group rely on your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

3a. Wellbeing check-ins: your consent

  • The first time you fill in a check-in, Ascend asks whether you agree to it being stored and synced with your account. Without that agreement, no check-in is stored.
  • Sharing with a group's staff is a second, separate agreement, given group by group (Sharing settings).
  • You can withdraw your agreement at any time in Settings → Privacy: your check-ins are then deleted from your account and immediately stop being visible to groups.
  • Ascend never uses this data for anything other than your own tracking and your staff's, when you have chosen so.

4. What never leaves your computer

  • Videos of your games, clips and thumbnails.
  • The Riot match cache and detailed statistics computed from it.
  • Debrief audio: transcription is done by a model running on your PC.
  • Personal Discord webhooks and per-PC settings (recording folder, quality, appearance).

5. Who sees what

  • Only you, by default.
  • A group's staff (coach, analyst, manager) sees your reviews, micro-reviews and statistics only if you share those categories with that group.
  • Your teammates see team data (schedule, scrims, drafts, published champion pools, meeting attendance, session feedback, weekly awards), never your personal reviews.
  • Without signing in: anyone who has a shared report link, the calendar subscription address or the team logo's address. The staff can revoke the first two at any time.
  • You can leave a group or withdraw sharing at any time; access stops immediately.

6. Hosting providers and recipients

  • Supabase: accounts, sync, backups, group features, team logos, shared reports and calendars. Hosted in the European Union / Switzerland (Zurich region).
  • Cloudflare: Riot Games API relay, web app, Discord bot and this website (global network).
  • Riot Games: receives the necessary API requests, under its own privacy policy.
  • Discord: only if you use that integration.
  • GitHub: hosts the installer files and application updates; this website queries GitHub's public API to show the latest version.

To limit calls to the Riot API, our relay keeps a shared cache of public League of Legends games it has already fetched, stored with Cloudflare (R2 storage). This match data comes from Riot Games and includes the Riot ID and Riot account identifier (PUUID) of the 10 players in each game, including players who have no Ascend account. It is used only to avoid requesting the same game from Riot again, and is deleted after 90 days.

No data is sold or used for advertising. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.

7. Retention

  • Cloud backups: the 10 most recent per account; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Account, synced data and group data: until you delete your account.
  • Wellbeing check-ins: until you withdraw your consent or delete your account.
  • Shared reports and calendar addresses: until the staff revokes them, or the group is deleted.
  • Riot relay technical logs: the hosting provider's standard retention; Ascend does not store the requests.
  • Relay cache of Riot games: 90 days.
  • Bug reports: until handled, and deleted on request.

When you leave a group: your access stops immediately and the group no longer sees your shared data (reviews, statistics, wellbeing). What you wrote for the group (comments, session feedback, schedule requests) and what the staff recorded about you (attendance, assigned goals) stay in the group, as part of its history. You can ask for their deletion at exciid501@gmail.com.

When you delete your account: your personal data is deleted: your account, synced data, backups, wellbeing check-ins, session feedback, sharing settings and Discord links. Your other contributions to groups (comments, recorded attendance, schedule items) stay in their history, but anonymized: your name no longer appears on them. If you own a group, that role passes to another member, in this order: a coach, otherwise a manager, an analyst, the captain, then the group's longest-standing player. The group is deleted only if no other member is left.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings → Backup exports all your data to a .nexusmind file.
  • Erasure: Settings → Ascend account → Delete my account immediately deletes your account, synced data and cloud backups (see § 7 for group data). Data on your PC is not touched: to erase it, uninstall the application, then delete its data folder (under %APPDATA%) and your recordings folder.
  • Withdrawing wellbeing consent: Settings → Privacy, effective immediately (§ 3a).
  • Rectification, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent: write to exciid501@gmail.com. We answer within one month.
  • Complaint: you can contact your country's data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

9. Minors

Ascend is intended for people aged 15 and over. Below that age, using an account requires the agreement of a parent or legal guardian. For wellbeing check-ins, the consent of a user under 15 must be given or authorized by that parent or guardian.

10. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), desktop session protected by the Windows keychain, row-level access control on the server (everyone only reads what is meant for or shared with them), Riot API key never present in the application. Public links (reports, calendar) rely on long random tokens: only share them with the people concerned.

11. Changes

This policy may change with the application. Significant changes will be announced in the app before they apply. Version 0.3 is one: it will be announced in the app the first time you open it after its publication.
