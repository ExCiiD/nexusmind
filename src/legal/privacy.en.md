## What Ascend stores, and where
**On your PC (by default).** Your sessions, reviews, scores, notes, video recordings and statistics stay in the app, on your computer.

**In the Ascend cloud, only if you have an account:**
- your e-mail address (code-based sign-in);
- your backups: a compressed copy of your coaching data (sessions, games, reviews, mistakes, teams, champion pools). Videos and the Riot match cache are never included;
- technical information about each backup (date, size, app version, computer name).

**When the app calls the Riot API**, it goes through an Ascend relay server (Cloudflare) that adds the Riot API key. The relay sees your Ascend account identifier and the request sent to Riot (for example your Riot ID or a match identifier). It does not keep these requests beyond the host's temporary technical logs.

## Why
- To provide the service: backup, restore on another PC, access to Riot data.
- To protect the service from abuse (per-account request limits).

Legal basis: performance of the service you request (GDPR art. 6.1.b).

## Hosting providers (processors)
- **Supabase**: accounts and backups, hosted in the European Union / Switzerland (Zurich region).
- **Cloudflare**: relay to the Riot API (global network).
- **Riot Games**: receives the necessary API requests (Riot ID, match identifiers), under its own privacy policy.

No data is sold or used for advertising.

## Retention
- Backups: the 10 most recent per account; older ones are deleted automatically.
- Account and backups: until you delete your account.

## Your rights
- **Access and portability**: *Settings → Backup* exports all your data to a `.nexusmind` file.
- **Erasure**: *Settings → Ascend account → Delete my account* immediately deletes your account and all your cloud backups. Data on your PC is not touched.
- **Rectification, objection, complaints**: write to [CONTACT E-MAIL]. You can also contact your country's data protection authority.
